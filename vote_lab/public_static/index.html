      The part that matters

      Proof has limits.

      Cryptography can expose inconsistent evidence. It cannot make an unsafe voting environment safe.

      Read the threat model
      Independent custody: inconclusive.

      The local shared-account isolation test failed. Real protection needs honest observers, separately authenticated keys, and evidence the operator cannot erase.

      01Compromised phones

      A device can change a vote or reveal it.

      02Coercion & vote buying

      A receipt does not remove pressure or prevent every form of proof.

      03Unseen suppression

      No trusted observation means no evidence of what was omitted.

      04Denial of service

      Proofs cannot keep a service available or give everyone access.
