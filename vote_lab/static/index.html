        Evidence has boundaries

        What this experiment cannot solve

        Cryptographic checks expose certain inconsistent records. They cannot make an unsafe voting environment safe.

        Compromised devices

        A malicious device can change a choice before encryption or expose it. This lab has no cast-as-intended mechanism.

        Coercion & vote buying

        A valid record does not prove that a voter acted freely. This prototype provides no coercion resistance.

        Denial of service

        Verification cannot restore access for voters who were blocked, excluded, or unable to participate.

        Compromised authorities

        Eligibility and secrecy depend on which keys and roles are compromised. Signed records prove possession of keys, not honest conduct.

        Compare authorization models
